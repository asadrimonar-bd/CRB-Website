What We Offer

Reproductive Services Grounded in Science

CRB supports veterinarians, technicians and farmers across the full reproductive management cycle — from fertility assessment to research-informed field solutions.

Artificial Insemination (AI)

Structured AI support built around accurate heat detection and correctly timed insemination to improve conception outcomes.

Learn More

Fertility Management

Herd-level fertility programs designed to improve conception rates, calving intervals and overall reproductive rhythm.

Learn More

Repeat Breeding Investigation & Solutions

Systematic investigation into repeat breeding cases, identifying underlying causes and practical corrective steps.

Learn More

Pregnancy Diagnosis

Reliable pregnancy diagnosis to support timely reproductive decisions and accurate herd records.

Learn More

Bull Fertility Evaluation

Evaluation of bull reproductive soundness to support informed breeding decisions.

Learn More

Semen Quality Testing

Laboratory-based assessment of semen quality to inform fertility and breeding decisions.

Learn More

Embryo Transfer Support

Technical support around embryo transfer procedures for advanced breeding programs.

Learn More

Training & Capacity Building

Structured training for veterinarians, AI technicians and students in reproductive management.

Research & Development

Evidence-based research into bovine fertility, reproductive health and biotechnology.
