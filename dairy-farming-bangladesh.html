Feeding, Nutrition & Fodder

Calf: 0–2 months

Colostrum management, clean milk/milk replacer strategy, calf starter, clean water, hygiene and gradual rumen development. Exact volumes should follow calf body weight and veterinary/nutrition guidance.

Growing heifer

Target steady frame growth, adequate protein/energy/minerals, forage quality and avoid excessive fatness before breeding.

Dry cow

Manage body condition, forage quality, mineral program and transition diet. Prevent sudden diet changes and over-conditioning.

Lactating cow

Balance forage, concentrate, protein, energy, effective fibre, minerals and water according to body weight, milk yield and stage of lactation.

বাংলাদেশে ব্যবহারযোগ্য fodder options

Napier cultivation workflow

  1. Well-drained fertile plot and clean planting material.
  2. Prepare land and use locally recommended spacing/manure/fertilizer.
  3. Plant cuttings/slips in suitable moisture; irrigate as needed.
  4. Weed control and periodic nutrient management.
  5. Harvest at an appropriate leafy stage; chop before feeding.
  6. Rotate plots to maintain year-round supply.

Reference: DLS Publications and Bangladesh Agricultural University research on Napier management. citeturn0search1turn0search14

Artificial Insemination & Reproductive Management

Heat detection

Standing to be mounted is a key sign. Also monitor restlessness, mounting activity, clear mucus, vulval changes, reduced feed intake or milk changes. Use multiple observations/day.

AI timing

Base AI on observed onset of standing heat and the farm's approved AI protocol. Record exact heat onset and service time. Do not inseminate solely from mucus without assessing heat.

Pregnancy management

Record service date, expected pregnancy-check window and outcome. If not pregnant, review heat detection, semen handling, technician technique, uterine/ovarian status, nutrition and disease before simply repeating AI.

Repeat breeder workflow

After repeated unsuccessful services, perform structured history + reproductive examination and investigate cow, bull/semen, technician, timing, nutrition and infectious/non-infectious causes.

AI practice should follow Bangladesh DLS/approved training and technician protocols. DLS publishes guidance related to artificial breeding implementation. citeturn0search0

Health, Vaccination & First Aid

Vaccination

Maintain a farm vaccination calendar based on DLS/local veterinary recommendations, disease risk, vaccine label and regional epidemiology. Keep batch, date, animal ID and next due date.

DLS provides vaccination-related guidance and disease-control materials, including LSD guidance. citeturn0search3turn0search8

First aid: diarrhoea

Isolate when infectious disease is possible, maintain hydration, continue appropriate nutrition, monitor temperature and dehydration. Calves can deteriorate quickly—seek veterinary help early.

First aid: bloat

Emergency. Stop access to the suspected feed, keep the animal calm and contact a veterinarian immediately—especially if breathing is difficult or the left flank is severely distended.

First aid: injury

Control bleeding with clean pressure, protect wound from contamination and seek veterinary assessment for deep wounds, fractures, severe lacerations or infection.

Mastitis suspicion

Abnormal milk, swollen/hot painful udder or systemic illness requires prompt veterinary assessment. Follow milk-withdrawal instructions for any medicine used.

Emergency red flags

Severe respiratory distress, recumbency, prolonged dystocia, severe bloat, poisoning, seizures, heavy bleeding, sudden death or multiple animals sick → isolate safely and contact veterinarian/DLS service urgently.

Antimicrobial stewardship: DLS published a Veterinary Antimicrobial Stewardship Guideline in 2026. Avoid casual antibiotic use and follow veterinary diagnosis, dose, duration and withdrawal requirements. citeturn0search0

Housing & Biosecurity

Ideal housing

Dry, well-drained floor; good ventilation without harmful drafts; adequate resting space; non-slip walking surface; clean feed/water points; manure drainage; shade and heat-stress mitigation.

Quarantine

New arrivals should be separated and observed/tested according to farm risk and veterinary plan before mixing.

Disease-free SOP

Source healthy animals → quarantine → vaccination/health verification → clean housing → controlled visitors → dedicated tools where possible → daily observation → rapid isolation → veterinary diagnosis → treatment and withdrawal records → controlled movement.

Calf area

Clean, dry, well-ventilated individual/group system appropriate to local welfare and disease-control needs; disinfect equipment and avoid mixing age groups unnecessarily.

About this app

Core principle: Observe → Record → Identify risk → Take safe first action → Call the right professional → Follow up.
