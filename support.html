Farmer & Veterinary Support

Practical Support for Better Reproductive Performance

CRB promotes practical, science-based reproductive management at the farm level — helping farmers and veterinarians act on the factors that matter most.

Heat DetectionRecognizing signs of oestrus accurately and consistently.
Timely AIEnsuring insemination happens within the optimal window.
Pregnancy DiagnosisConfirming pregnancy status promptly to guide herd decisions.
Reproductive Record KeepingMaintaining accurate records to track reproductive performance over time.
Repeat Breeder ManagementIdentifying and addressing recurring breeding failures.
Body Condition & Reproductive ManagementManaging body condition as a foundation for reproductive success.
Herd Fertility MonitoringOngoing monitoring of reproductive indicators across the herd.
Farm Reproductive Performance AssessmentStructured assessment of reproductive performance at farm level.

Our Approach

Science-Based, Farm-Ready Guidance

Every recommendation from CRB is grounded in reproductive science and shaped to be practical at the farm level — so that better reproductive outcomes are within reach for every herd, regardless of size.
