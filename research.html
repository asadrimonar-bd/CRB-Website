Research & Development

Evidence-Based Cattle Reproduction Research

CRB focuses on research that improves reproductive performance in Bangladeshi cattle through practical, scientifically grounded approaches to fertility, artificial insemination and reproductive health.

Research Focus

Key Areas of Cattle Reproduction Research

Our research interests cover the reproductive cycle from biological mechanisms of fertility to herd-level management factors that influence conception, pregnancy maintenance and reproductive efficiency.

Our Approach

Science That Serves the Field

CRB research is designed to translate scientific evidence into practical value for veterinarians, AI technicians, researchers and cattle producers.

✓Evidence FirstResearch questions and recommendations are grounded in reproductive science and measurable evidence.
✓Field-RelevantResearch priorities reflect real reproductive challenges encountered in cattle farms and AI programs.
✓CollaborativeCRB welcomes collaboration with universities, laboratories, research institutions and livestock professionals.
