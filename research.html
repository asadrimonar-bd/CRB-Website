Research & Development

Evidence-Based Investigation Into Cattle Reproduction

CRB's research work is directed at understanding and improving reproductive outcomes in Bangladeshi cattle herds, using scientific language made accessible to practitioners and producers alike.

Focus Areas

Research Topics

Our research interests span the reproductive cycle — from the biological mechanisms behind fertility to the practical, herd-level factors that determine reproductive success.

Repeat Breeding Syndrome Bovine Fertility Reproductive Health Artificial Insemination Pregnancy Loss Bull Fertility Semen Quality Research Reproductive Biotechnology Herd Reproductive Performance Evidence-Based Reproductive Management

Our Approach

Science That Serves the Field

Research at CRB is designed to translate directly into better outcomes for veterinarians, technicians and farmers.

Evidence FirstRecommendations grounded in reproductive science, not assumption.
Field-RelevantResearch questions shaped by real challenges seen on farms.
CollaborativeOpen to partnership with universities and research institutions.
