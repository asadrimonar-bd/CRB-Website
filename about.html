About CRB

Advancing Bovine Reproductive Health in Bangladesh

Cattle Reproduction Bangladesh is focused on advancing bovine reproductive health and reproductive efficiency through science, technology, research, education and professional collaboration.

Our Vision

To become a leading centre of excellence in cattle reproduction, reproductive biotechnology and livestock fertility solutions in Bangladesh.

Our Mission

To improve cattle fertility and reproductive productivity through evidence-based reproductive technologies, research, professional education and practical field solutions.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

Scientific ExcellenceGrounding every recommendation in sound reproductive science.
IntegrityHonest, evidence-based guidance without exaggerated claims.
InnovationApplying modern reproductive technologies to local conditions.
EducationBuilding lasting capacity among vets, technicians and farmers.
CollaborationWorking alongside professionals and institutions across the sector.
Farmer FocusKeeping practical, farm-level impact at the centre of our work.
SustainabilitySupporting a livestock sector that stays productive long-term.
