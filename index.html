Why It Matters

Why Cattle Reproduction Matters

Reproductive efficiency sits at the centre of every productive herd. A shorter calving interval, timely conception and lower rates of repeat breeding directly shape how many calves are born, how much milk a herd produces across its lifetime, and how efficiently a farm can replace and grow its herd.

When reproductive performance declines, the effects move quickly through a farm's economics — extended dry periods, delayed herd replacement and reduced lifetime productivity. CRB works to strengthen this foundation through evidence-based reproductive management, so that farms, veterinarians and technicians can build herds that are healthier, more fertile and more sustainable over time.
